# AI-assisted data analysis, with a human check

An experiment: can AI run a full data analysis end to end, and can the result be trusted in front of senior leaders?

AI did all of the work here — profiling the file, running the statistics, writing the findings, building the dashboard. It was fast, the output looked finished, and the arithmetic was correct throughout.

That last part is the reason the question matters. Correct arithmetic is not the same as a safe conclusion. What fourteen years in regulatory audit taught me to look for is the part the numbers never announce: a figure calculated over the wrong group of records, a difference that is really just random variation, a chart that pushes a reader toward a decision the data does not support.

So I put that experience in the loop as the control. Nothing reached the dashboard until it had been independently challenged and verified against the source file. **Six claims did not survive that check. None of them were maths errors.**

What I wanted to test was the division of labour: AI produces the analysis, and human experience decides what is accurate enough to put in front of a decision-maker.

> **The dataset is synthetic**, generated for this exercise. No employer or production data, and nothing here describes a real organisation.
